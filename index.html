Finance Sales Performance Dashboard

Power BIPower QueryDAX

A four-page Power BI report on a finance sales dataset — one model covering sales performance, customers, products and transactions.

finance-sales-dashboard.pdf
Power BI · Power Query · DAX · Data Modeling
Problem

Sales, customer, product and transaction data sat in separate tables, so totals, margin and customer behaviour could not be compared in one view.

Solution

Related the tables in Power BI, shaped them in Power Query and wrote DAX measures for net sales, COGS, profit, profit margin and running totals — then built four pages: Sales Overview, Customer Analysis, Product Analysis and Transaction Analysis.

Result

127.93M gross sales, 118.73M net sales and 16.89M profit at a 14.23% margin on 1.13M units sold. Government is the largest segment at 44.22% of net sales while Enterprise runs at a loss of 6.14L, and the United States leads net sales at 25.0M.

Blinkit SQL Analysis

SQLMySQL

A MySQL analysis of Blinkit's quick-commerce database — customers, sales, delivery and inventory answered entirely in SQL.

blinkit-sql-analysis.pdf
MySQL · Joins · CTEs · Window Functions
Problem

Customer, order, product and inventory data lived in five related tables with no reporting layer, so every sales, delivery or stock question had to be written from the raw rows.

Solution

Joined the five tables on their keys and answered each question in SQL — GROUP BY aggregations, DATE_FORMAT time grouping, subqueries, CTEs and DENSE_RANK for Top-N, plus TIMESTAMPDIFF for delivery gaps.

Result

Top 10 customers, products and categories by revenue; customer growth by year and area-wise distribution; delivery status, payment methods and delivery-time performance; and stock health measured as received versus damaged stock against min/max levels.

View Project ↗ Download PDF

A quick-commerce database with customers, orders, order items, products and inventory. Every business question is answered in SQL alone — no BI tool, no spreadsheet.

Inside the project

  • Five tables: customers, orders, order items, products, inventory
  • Four layers — customer, sales, delivery & payment, inventory
  • Top-10 customers, products and categories by revenue
  • Customer growth by year and customers per delivery area
  • Delivery status, payment method and delivery-time gaps
  • Stock received versus damaged, with min/max levels

How it was built

  1. Explored each table and confirmed the join keys
  2. Joined customers to orders, order items and products for the sales path
  3. Joined products to inventory for stock analysis
  4. Aggregated with GROUP BY, COUNT, SUM and ROUND
  5. Grouped time with DATE_FORMAT and measured gaps with TIMESTAMPDIFF
  6. Ranked results with DENSE_RANK inside CTEs and subqueries

Web ScrapingPower QueryPower BI

icc-cricket-dashboard.pdf

E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

ExcelPower Query

An Excel dashboard on 100 e-commerce orders — revenue, discounts, returns and regional performance on a single page.

ecommerce-dashboard.pdf
Excel · Power Query · PivotTables · Slicers
Problem

Order-level data sat in a flat file with no single view of revenue, returns or regional performance, so totals had to be recalculated by hand.

Solution

Cleaned the orders in Power Query, then built an Excel dashboard with PivotTables and charts for region, category and month, plus slicers on region, category and return flag.

Result

₹14.28L revenue across 100 orders and 100 customers, with a 25% return rate and ₹77.62 average discount. East leads the regions at ₹3.72L ahead of West (₹3.23L) and South (₹2.81L).
